Top 6 Passive Income Checklist — Students

1) Refer local clients
- Pick 5 local businesses (salon, tutor, café)
- Use the WhatsApp pitch: "Hi [Name], I noticed your [business/page] — a simple website could bring more enquiries. I can connect you with a team who builds fast and you pay after delivery. Interested?"
- Introduce via WhatsApp/email and stay in the thread until kickoff

2) Sell a small digital product
- Create 1 asset (resume template, study notes, Instagram planner)
- Price ₹99–₹499 and list on Gumroad or a microsite
- Promote in college groups and Insta stories

3) Print-on-demand (POD)
- Make 5–10 niche designs, connect to Printful/Teespring
- Post short videos to drive orders

4) Stock photos & UGC clips
- Shoot campus life, study spaces, flatlays
- Upload to microstock sites and UGC marketplaces

5) Short-form content + referral funnels
- Make 30–60s videos about study hacks or tools
- Add a link-in-bio landing page and track clicks

6) Micro-templates / no-code components
- Build a Notion resume template or mini microsite template
- Price between ₹299–₹1,499 depending on value

Quick promo checklist
- Publish article + pin social post
- Post 3 short videos and 3 shareable images
- Seed to 5 targeted communities (WhatsApp groups / Reddit / Telegram)

Use the above as a one-page checklist to start your first experiment this week.
